Privacy policy · Updated October 9, 2026

Privacy, in plain language.

Deviation Lens is a browser extension that scans a page for supported image files when you open or rescan the extension.

Information the extension processes

The extension reads image URLs, image dimensions, page headings, and related page details in the active tab to find and filter image results. Scan results are processed in the browser and are not sent to a developer-operated server. When you enable “Find images on any page,” Chrome asks you to grant access to each additional site you scan.

Information stored locally

Preferences such as Explore filtering, image display options, and download mode are stored using Chrome extension storage. If you connect a custom folder, its access handle is stored locally in the extension’s browser database. The extension does not include analytics, advertising, or an account service.

Downloads and third-party services

When you download an image or inspect its file size, the browser requests the image from its source host. Those requests are governed by the source host’s privacy practices. The extension does not control how DeviantArt or image hosts process their own requests.

Children and changes

Deviation Lens is a general browser utility and does not knowingly collect personal information. This policy may be updated if the extension’s behavior changes; the effective date above will be revised.

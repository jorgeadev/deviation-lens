# Code of conduct

People contributing to Deviation Lens are expected to make participation respectful and safe for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.
- Critique ideas and code constructively. Do not attack or demean people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and correct mistakes without hostility.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else’s personal information or private communications.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention without consent, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or a maintainer using a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive reports in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports and may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or remove someone from project spaces. They will consider the impact and context of the behavior. They will protect the reporter’s privacy as far as practical and share details only as needed to review the report.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue and pull request discussions, and project-related public spaces.
